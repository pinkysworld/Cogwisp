Cogwisp

Connect gears and belts. Bring little workshop machines to life.

A spark, a crank, and a machine waiting to run again. In Cogwisp, route mechanical power through a warm workshop of wood and brass. Rotate parts, move the movable pieces, and find a path from the source to every target.

Coming soon to Windows and AndroidMicrosoft Store and Google Play – Cogwisp has not been released yet.

How it works

A connection is only the beginning: targets need the right rotation and speed. Gears and crossed belts reverse direction, while clutches engage or disconnect a drive. Later, freewheels and cams add new problems, including working phases to coordinate. Step through a cycle or use Test run to see the movement unfold.

A look inside

Your workshop

  • 36 handcrafted puzzle rooms across six chapters.
  • Tap to rotate; drag to move or swap movable parts.
  • Unlimited undo during a play session, plus a room reset you can undo.
  • Three progressive hints per room and a separately available solution.
  • Progress and unfinished puzzles saved locally.
  • English and German, switchable in the menu.
  • No ads.

Free prologue, buy the full game once

The six prologue rooms are free to play. Unlock the 30 further rooms through a single in-app purchase. No subscription.

Puzzles and a full-game unlock already saved locally can be played offline. Purchases and purchase restoration require Google Play and an internet connection.
