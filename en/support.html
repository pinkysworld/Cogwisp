Help & Support

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to mip@gmx.biz. Device model, Android version and – for a puzzle – chapter and room are helpful.

Where is my progress saved?

Only locally on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, your progress is gone.

How do I restore my purchase?

After reinstalling, or on another device with the same Google account, open the purchase page in the app and choose restore. This requires Google Play and an internet connection. A full-game unlock already saved locally also works offline.

How do I change the language?

Cogwisp is available in English and German. You can switch the language in the menu.

What does Cogwisp cost?

The six prologue rooms are free. Unlock the 30 further rooms with a single in-app purchase. No subscription, no ads.

I am stuck in a room.

Each room has three progressive hints and a separately available solution. Step-through and Test run show how the parts move.
